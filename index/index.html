Synth prototype map

Fourteen small builds of the crafting game, each playable on its own. Every one was played by a script before it was published; the notes say what worked and what did not.

If you play one, play 14. It is the whole loop: gather, ask for a design, build, sleep, get raided, salvage. It keeps your game, its raids are the best so far, and the night no longer makes you wait. Then try 10 or 11 to feel what scarce materials do to it.

The builds

  1. 01Physics toy

    Nodes, bonds, water, the hand. Does a bucket carry water?

    WorksBucket keeps over 70% of its water from pond to ditch. Needed one rule the brief does not have (a straight run of three stays straight).

  2. 02Gather and craft

    Sources, inventory, costs, letter-grid recipes, a recipe book.

    Works, dullRecipes round-trip exactly. Gathering is click and wait, and nothing is scarce.

  3. 03Synthesizer

    Talk to a helper, get a draft, test it on a bench, build it.

    WorksTen scripted requests: 10 valid drawings and 8 passing their physical test from a Claude model, 9 from the offline helper. Asks Claude on your own account, no key. The live call still needs a person to try it.

  4. 04Creature pen

    Bodies with a heart and pulsing sinew. Do they walk, eat, die, leave salvage?

    WorksSix seed crawlers cross the ground; three were bred to eat as well as walk. Drawing your own by hand almost never walks.

  5. 05Night raid

    Creatures bred overnight against a copy of your base, then a raid.

    Passes its gateAgainst a low wall, the best score rose over five nights in 4 of 5 runs, by breaking through or shoving. Raids are hit and miss.

  6. 06Day and night

    Everything together, with daylight as a clock.

    Too easyOne wall from the recipe book stops every raid. No arms race.

  7. 07Siege

    Raiders also scored on damage to what you built.

    PressureThe wall wears down night after night. Also fixed here: a raider now does in the raid exactly what it did in its trial.

  8. 08Big beasts

    Bigger bodies, minute-long raids, the dead last one day, a score and an ending.

    A threatLeft alone, one wall falls and the stash is lost within about a week of nights. Pitch on the ground used to trap raiders for good; fixed in every build.

  9. 09Saved games

    08 that remembers world, burrow, inventory and score across reloads.

    WorksReload brings back every node and all 16 kinds in the burrow; the next night runs normally.

  10. 10Daily crop

    Sources drop a finite crop of loose pieces each dawn.

    PromisingMaterials are scarce for the first time: a wall is nearly half a day's wood. Daylight may no longer matter.

  11. 11Haul it home

    Pieces only count once carried home.

    Promising, fiddlyCarrying the tree's crop by hand is half a day. A pail carries two or three trips' worth, but loading it is awkward.

  12. 12Families

    09 where the burrow keeps one best creature per founding seed and size.

    Better raidsEvery party is three different animals. In the browser, with nobody repairing: wall down on night 4, stash eaten on night 5, ending card.

  13. 13The burrow grows

    12 where the night tries 100 more bodies each time.

    BalanceA scripted defender who rebuilds one wall every morning holds 12 nights against a steady burrow, and loses the stash by night 7 against a growing one. Costs a longer wait each night.

  14. 14Day shift

    12 where the burrow breeds in the background while you work, then a short night.

    Best pacingNights take about 6 seconds and a scripted run still loses the stash on night 6. Headless, it beats a wall rebuilt every morning in 2 of 3 seeds. How much the burrow gets done depends on how fast your machine is.

Waiting on you

  • Yes or no on five changes to the brief: the straightness rule (01), raiders scored on wrecking (07), dead raiders vanishing after a day (08), pitch gripping things less than it grips the ground, and the ditch moved so it no longer sits between burrow and home.
  • Open 03 or 14 on claude.ai, switch the helper to Claude and ask for something. That call has only been tested from recordings.
  • Play 14 for ten minutes. Every finding here comes from a script; none comes from a person.

Where the detail is
