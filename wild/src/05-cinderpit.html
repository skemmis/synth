Cinderpit

Cribbed from falling-sand toys and Noita. Every grain follows two or three tiny rules: water flows, oil floats and burns, moss drinks water and grows, bangdust goes off, acid eats rock. Six sealed chambers hold pellets. Nothing here is a bomb or a pump until you pour the right things next to each other. Get the pellets down to the glowing floor using as few grains as you can.

What to try
  • Pellets float on water and sink in oil. Fill an open cup with water and they bob up and over the rim.
  • A spark on moss burns a plug out. A spark on oil burns the whole pool. A spark on bangdust takes the wall with it.
  • Lay a fuse. A trail of moss or oil leading to a pile of bangdust lets you light it from a safe distance, and bangdust sets off other bangdust.
  • Acid is the lazy answer and costs the most grains. Try clearing a pit without it.
  • In the empty pit, pour water on moss and leave it for a minute.
