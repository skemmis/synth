Twitch

Gulp's soft body with Wirehead's wiring. The cell has four patches on its membrane, A to D. Each patch has a nose and a tuft of cilia. A pellet nose fires when it smells more pellet than the other three do, an urchin nose fires when an urchin is near, and a firing tuft shoves the cell away from its own side. You do not steer. You decide which nose fires which tuft, then watch one cell work the cave for 40 seconds.

Rows are what a patch senses. Columns are whose cilia fire. Click to cycle nothing, +, −.

What to try
  • Start from Blank. Nothing moves. Wire "A smells pellet" to C's cilia with +. C is opposite A, so its shove carries the cell toward what A smells. Do the same for the other three.
  • It gets stuck behind rocks, because smell goes through rock and the cell does not. Wire the slow pulse to one tuft and see if the wobble frees it.
  • Urchins cost points. Wire each urchin nose to its own patch's cilia so the cell backs off.
  • Press Breed (it takes a few seconds) and read what it changed.
