Bloomfield

Cribbed from Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion, the two-chemical recipe behind leopard spots and coral. Your organism is not a thing, it is a stain that keeps itself alive. On bare ground it holds as quiet spots. Where you lay feed it grows, branches and splits. Lead it to the pellets before your feed runs out.

What to try
  • Draw a thin feed line from the stain to the nearest pellet. The stain eats the line as it follows it, so thin lines go further than fat ones.
  • Each pellet reached refunds feed and blooms on the spot. Chain them.
  • Walls starve whatever touches them. Use one to cut a branch off, or to stop the bloom spreading the wrong way.
  • Change the ground to "Dividing cells" and drop one spore. Now it spreads with no feed at all and the game becomes containment.
