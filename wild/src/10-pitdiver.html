Pitdiver

Gulp's soft cell dropped into Cinderpit's grain world. The same grains, the same two or three rules each, but now you are inside it and soft. You cannot pour from the sky. You squirt one material from your own membrane, toward the pointer, out of a small store of juice that pellets refill. Fire and acid leak you. A blast throws you.

What to try
  • The cell follows the pointer. Swim up under the chamber with the green moss plug, pick Spark, and squirt at the plug. Then get out from under what falls.
  • Squirt water into an open cup from above and the pellets float up to you.
  • Bangdust is a tool and a mistake. Lay a little against a stone wall, back off, and light it with one spark from as far away as your squirt reaches.
  • Acid opens a vault quietly but any that drips on you costs girth.
  • Getting fat is a problem again. Holes you blast are only so wide.
