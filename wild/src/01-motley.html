Motley

Cribbed from Particle Life. Five colours of mote, and one small table that says how much each colour likes or avoids each other colour. Nothing else. Bodies, chasers and shells form on their own. Only the pale motes can smell a pellet, and they cannot crack it. Cracking takes three coloured motes touching it at once, so a body has to follow its noses.

Row colour feels this way about column colour. Click to step up, right-click or Shift-click to step down.

What to try
  • Make a chaser. Set teal to like coral (+1) and coral to avoid teal (−1). One colour hunts the other forever and the pair starts to travel.
  • Start from Blank. The pale noses find pellets and sit on them, useless. Now give one colour a reason to follow pale (+1 in the pale column) and the count starts.
  • Press "New random rules" a dozen times. Roughly one in five gives something that looks alive. "Nudge rules" changes one number, which is how you breed one you like.
  • Drag in the dish to stir. Good bodies heal, bad ones never re-form.
