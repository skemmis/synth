Gaitkeeper

Cribbed from Sodarace and Karl Sims' evolved creatures. You build the body out of joints, bones and muscles. You do not get to say how the muscles fire. Breeding tries hundreds of rhythms and keeps whichever carries the body furthest to the right in seven seconds, past the most pellets.

What to try
  • Press "Let it go" first. With untrained muscles most bodies shuffle on the spot. Then press "Breed a gait" and watch the same body walk.
  • Add one joint to a bred body and breed again. A tail, a heavy head or a long leg changes the gait completely.
  • Click a link with the middle tool to turn a bone into a muscle (coral), remove it, or bring it back. Fewer muscles often go further.
  • With "Add joint" selected, click empty space for a new joint that ties itself to its neighbours, or drag from one joint to another to tie those two.
  • Try to beat 600 with eight joints or fewer.
