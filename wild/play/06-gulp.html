Gulp

Cribbed from pressure soft bodies (the jelly blobs of Box2D demos and the Spore cell stage). You are a bag of fluid: a ring of beads joined by springs, held round by pressure. That is the whole body. It squeezes through gaps because nothing says it cannot. Every pellet makes you bigger, and bigger does not fit. Spit mass out to shrink and get a kick forward.

What to try
  • Move the pointer (or drag on a phone) and the cell swims after it. Push into a gap narrower than you are and keep pushing.
  • Eat five pellets, then try the same gap. Spit to shrink. What you spit can be eaten again later, so park it somewhere open.
  • Spit while wedged. The kick is the only fast move you have.
  • Coral urchins leak you. A leak sprays pellets you can re-eat if you get clear.
  • Switch the membrane to Slack or Taut and feel how much of the game was in that one number.
