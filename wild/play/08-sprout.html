Sprout

Cribbed from Cell Lab and from Karl Sims' idea that a body is a small program that unfolds. You do not build the creature. You write four cell types: what job each does, when it splits, and what its two halves become. One germ cell is dropped in and the body grows itself. The dish wraps around at the edges. Mouths eat pellets, leaves soak up light, tails push against whatever they are joined to, and joined cells share food.

What to try
  • Start from "Tadpole" and read its four cards against what you see. Then change one dropdown and drop a fresh germ.
  • Start from "Leaf raft", a sheet that never lets go of its halves. Make one half a tail and see whether the sheet can afford to move.
  • Two tails on one small mouth pull against each other and starve it. Most bodies you write will die. The ones that live are worth keeping.
  • Leaves need no pellets but earn slowly and shade each other. See whether a leaf body can afford a tail.
  • Aim for the most cells alive at once (the dish holds 160), then for the most pellets a minute. They want different bodies.
  • Click the dish to drop pellets where you want them.
