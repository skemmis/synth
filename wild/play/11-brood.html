Brood

Cribbed from The Bibites and other artificial life ponds, using Wirehead's swimmers. Nobody wires anything here. A swimmer that eats enough pellets splits in two, and each half gets a slightly miscopied wiring. One that runs out of energy dies. You are the weather: you decide where food falls and where the poison sits, and the pond rewires itself around what you do.

The average wiring of everything alive right now. It starts as noise.

What to try
  • Leave it at 4x for two minutes and watch the table. The crossed pellet wires usually turn green by themselves.
  • Turn the rain to zero and feed by hand in one corner only. See how long it takes the pond to learn where the corner is, and what it does when you move.
  • Put poison on the food. Feed only inside a poison cloud and see whether anything learns to dash in and out.
  • Colour is family. Each miscopy shifts the hue a little, so a pond of one colour is one lineage that won.
