Wirehead

Cribbed from Braitenberg's vehicles, with a breeding button borrowed from The Bibites. Each swimmer has two antennae that smell pellets and poison, and two cilia at the back. There is no brain except the wires you connect between them. Eight copies of your wiring swim each 40 second round.

What to try
  • Cross the pellet wires. Left antenna to right cilium, right antenna to left cilium, both +. The swimmer now turns toward what it smells. Uncross them and it turns away.
  • Add poison wires the uncrossed way so it veers off. Then try making poison a brake (−) instead and see which scores better.
  • The pulse is a slow beat. Wire it to one cilium only and the swimmer searches in loops when it smells nothing.
  • Press "Breed from my wiring" and compare what 15 generations of mutation came up with. The numbers it finds are often not the ones you would draw.
  • Click in the dish to drop a pellet in front of a swimmer.
