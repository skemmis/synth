Goo parts sandbox

Four kinds of atom and four kinds of link. Each does one plain thing. Build something, press Run, and see what it does in the water.

Tool
Atom
Link

Atoms

  • Bead does nothing. It is weight and something to push water with.
  • Jitter tugs every spring and rope it holds, a quick pull then a slow release. Jitters fire one after another in the order you placed them. Each beat spends energy; the ring around it is what's left.
  • Sticky glues itself to anything it touches, up to three things.
  • Pellet is loose energy. Touch one with any build that has a jitter in it and the jitters are fed.

Links

  • Rod keeps its length. Three in a triangle make something solid.
  • Spring stretches a long way and pulls back.
  • Rope pulls when taut and goes slack when not.
  • Fuse is stiff until it is stretched a little, then it snaps and kicks its two ends apart.

Every link keeps the length you built it at.

Starter builds

Build: click the water to place an atom; it links to the nearest atoms within reach (the faint lines show which). Drag from one atom to another to add a link between them. Grab: drag things around. Erase: click an atom or a link.
