Universe specimens

Atoms here have only raw properties: weight, drag, how many bonds they can hold, how they push and pull each other, and how their bonds form, stretch, let go and kick. Nothing was told to swim. A search tried about two million rule sets and bodies and kept the ones that move themselves through still water. Pick a specimen, watch it, then poke it.

Drop:

    Drag any atom to pull it. Pick a colour, then click empty water to drop an atom of that type. Right-click (or long-press) an atom to remove it.
